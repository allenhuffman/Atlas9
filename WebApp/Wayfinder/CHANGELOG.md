# Changelog — Unofficial Atlas9 Wayfinder

All notable changes to the Wayfinder app (the wayfinding-only Atlas9 app,
live at https://parkhopping.com/a9nav/).

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
Dates are the day each version went live. Versions before 1.2.0 are not in the
app's in-app release notes; 1.1.1 is included from the launch record.

## [1.4.23] - 2026-11-20

- Door notes now read as full phrases ("through the…") throughout.

## [1.4.22] - 2026-11-20

- Route steps now show the Door notes from the sheet — which door to take, under the step it describes.

## [1.4.21] - 2026-11-20

- The update bar now pops up over the title bar instead of pushing the page down, with Update and Dismiss buttons — dismissing hides it until a newer version appears.

## [1.4.20] - 2026-11-20

- Removed the direct Mesmerite–Jazz Club link — the route now goes through the Hypnotizer Room.

## [1.4.19] - 2026-11-20

- The update bar now sits right under the app name, in the fixed header.
- Room pickers and search results are now one A–Z list. Config has a "Separate rooms by floor" switch if you prefer the Floor 1 / Floor 2 groups.

## [1.4.18] - 2026-11-20

- "I am in" and "Or go to a room" now end with "…" like "How do I get to…".
- The shortcut buttons are now alphabetical: Elevator, Exit, FAZR, Food, Restroom, Stairs.

## [1.4.17] - 2026-11-20

- Your starting room and Config settings are now saved on this phone — Spoilers still starts off every time.

## [1.4.16] - 2026-11-20

- Config now says settings are not saved anywhere — they reset each time the app closes.

## [1.4.15] - 2026-11-20

- The Release Notes sheet now has a Check for updates button.

## [1.4.14] - 2026-11-20

- Added the Hypnotizer Room to Sapphire Jazz Club connection.
- Letter Hallway description added.

## [1.4.13] - 2026-11-20

- Added Letter Hallway, between StarBot and Theater Upper.

## [1.4.12] - 2026-11-20

- Fixed sheets opening scrolled down.

## [1.4.11] - 2026-11-20

- Choosing a destination now clears the "Or go to a room" search, so stale results stop pushing the route down.
- Sheets always open at the top now.

## [1.4.10] - 2026-11-20

- Grab bar removed from sheets — the × at the top-right is the way to close.
- Tapping the version number opens the release notes.

## [1.4.9] - 2026-11-20

- Every sheet now has a × at the top-right, so you can close from the top without scrolling to the bottom.

## [1.4.8] - 2026-11-20

- Release notes corrected.

## [1.4.7] - 2026-11-20

- Removing Key of Color (not a real room).

## [1.4.6] - 2026-11-20

- Report now gives you a heads-up if the report does not match what the app lists — for example, saying a Computer is "there but not listed" in a room that already lists one. It never blocks you: a room can hold a second object of the same type.

## [1.4.5] - 2026-11-20

- After you pick a room in "I am in", the button stays at the top of the screen so you can see the room you picked — before, the view jumped down to the shortcut buttons.
- The route now sits at the bottom of the screen, after "Or go to a room", so the page reads top to bottom: where you are, what you want, which room, then the route.

## [1.4.4] - 2026-11-20

- Every room chooser is now a picker followed by a search: "I am in", "Or go to a room" and Report all start with the room picker, and the long list of every room is gone — type in the search box to find a room by name.
- The shortcut heading is now "How do I get to…" — the old heading claimed the route was the quickest, which the app cannot know.

## [1.4.3] - 2026-11-20

- The route box button is now Close: it clears the route, puts all the shortcut buttons back to normal, and takes you back to the top.
- The header is shorter — the "Wayfinding only — no dossier." line is gone.
- Report now starts with a room picker, so you can report something in a different room than the one you are standing in.

## [1.4.2] - 2026-11-20

- Elevator and Exit now stay highlighted in gold when you tap them, like the other buttons — before, they routed without showing they were selected.
- Tapping a FAZR type now lists every room holding one, so you can pick the exact room instead of just the nearest.

## [1.4.1] - 2026-11-20

- A category tap now highlights only Nearest, even when the nearest room is also listed as a specific option — before, both lit up at once.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-20

- Tapping a category now routes to the nearest one right away — Stairs, Food, Restroom and FAZR show the nearest route at once, and the sub-buttons refine it from there.
- New Share icon up top: a QR code, a link to copy, or your phone's share sheet.
- New flag icon up top: report a FAZR, Computer, Movie Poster or INTERFAZE that is listed but not there, or there but not listed. It opens the report form pre-filled.
- Tapping FAZR no longer highlights both Nearest and FAZR at once — they are separate options now.

## [1.3.7] - 2026-11-20

- The sub-option you tapped now stays highlighted in gold, like the category button above it, so you can see at a glance which option the route is showing. Picking a room from the search list clears the highlight.

## [1.3.6] - 2026-11-20

- Tapping a shortcut no longer scrolls the route to the top — the route appears right below the buttons, so you can tap through the options and glance at each one. Picking a room from the search list still scrolls to the route.

## [1.3.5] - 2026-11-20

- The six shortcut buttons now fit in two rows of three, with Food/Drink shortened to Food.
- The What's new sheet no longer has its own "Got it" button — the sheet's Close button does the job.

## [1.3.4] - 2026-11-20

- Route steps now stay on screen as you walk: steps you have reached keep their numbers and are marked checked, and tapping one takes you back there.
- Tapping a step no longer jumps the screen — the list stays where it is. Only picking a new destination moves the route to the top.
- The six shortcut buttons are now just their names: Stairs, Elevator, Food/Drink, Restroom, Exit, FAZR.

## [1.3.3] - 2026-11-20

- Tapping a step now always lands the top of the route box at the top of the screen — the page has room to scroll there now.

## [1.3.2] - 2026-11-20

- Route steps are now numbered, so you can see which stop you are on while you walk.
- Tapping a step now lands the route title at the top of the screen instead of jumping under the header.
- Route steps no longer show a chevron — the number shows the order.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-11-20

- Spoilers no longer names anything hidden — About and the footer now just say it enables hidden routes and hidden items.
- Config has a new Include crawls switch: routes can use the crawl passages. Off by default, just for fun.
- Release Notes now live at the bottom of About, and a What's new sheet appears once after each update.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-20

- Shortcuts are now two-tier: pick a category, then Nearest or a specific target — including all four staircases and all five FAZR types.
- New Spoilers button up top. It enables hidden routes and hidden items, and it is off on every launch.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-20

- Config button up top, with Avoid stairs inside it.
- New shortcuts: Food/Drink and INTERFAZE.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-19

- First version. 50 pickable rooms, public connections only (no spoilers, crawls, or Headshot Remixer); "I am in" picker, Avoid stairs toggle, nearest stairs/elevator/restrooms/Exit shortcuts, searchable room list.
