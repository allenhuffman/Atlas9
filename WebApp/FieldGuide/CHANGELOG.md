# Changelog — Unofficial Atlas9 Field Guide

All notable changes to the FieldGuide app (the spreadsheet version of the
Atlas9 mobile fan guide, live at https://parkhopping.com/a9/).

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
Dates are the day each version went live. Versions before 1.9.3 predate this
repository — the app's earlier history lives in the V0 prototype snapshot
(`FieldGuide-V0/`, version 1.8.18).

## [1.17.20] - 2026-11-20

- The room picker lists are sorted A–Z within each floor now.
- Mesmerite no longer links straight to the Sapphire Jazz Club — that was old info; the route runs through the Hypnotizer Room now.

## [1.17.19] - 2026-11-20

- Crawls are passages, not rooms: report the room at the far end of a crawl and the crawl entrance opens up — no more picking the crawl itself as the destination.

## [1.17.18] - 2026-11-20

- Phones and Numbers in Researcher now read "X of ? found" with spoilers off, like FAZR and Passage — the real total shows with spoilers on.

## [1.17.17] - 2026-11-20

- The update bar now pops up over the title bar instead of sitting inside the header, with Update and Dismiss buttons — dismissing hides it until a newer version appears.
- Phone and number counts now read "X of ? found" with spoilers off (the real total shows with spoilers on). Rooms, people and timeline still show their totals, since those lists show everything.

## [1.17.16] - 2026-11-20

- The update bar now sits right under the app name, in the fixed header.
- After a passage report, the button says "Back to Passage".
- Counts now read "X of ? found" with spoilers off (the real total shows with spoilers on), and say "you found them all!" when a list is complete.

## [1.17.15] - 2026-11-20

- Found secret passages now have a checkmark, like everything else — tap it to unmark one.
- Config is here now, next to Spoilers: it has a Reset all button that erases everything saved on this device so you can start over.

## [1.17.14] - 2026-11-20

- The Passage tab now matches the other lists: "N of ? secret passages found" (the real total shows with spoilers on) and the yellow Report a Secret Passage box.

## [1.17.13] - 2026-11-20

- The Passage tab now works like the other lists: your found passages, then a "Found one not on your list yet?" box — notes you type there ride along into the report.
- Fixed the stray " — " at the start of FAZR and phone reports.

## [1.17.12] - 2026-11-20

- The Passage tab now shows the secret passages you found, with a Report a Secret Passage button at the top.

## [1.17.11] - 2026-11-20

- No more Profile screen — every report now asks for your name and contact, and remembers them for next time.

## [1.17.10] - 2026-11-20

- The Profile screen now says an app update can reset your saved data — that is expected, not a bug.

## [1.17.9] - 2026-11-20

- Tapping Update now shows what changed in the new version.

## [1.17.8] - 2026-11-20

- The Release Notes sheet now has a Check for updates button.

## [1.17.7] - 2026-11-20

- Reporting a number now uses the room picker — ring picker and search — instead of typing the room.
- The Report tab is now Passage: it goes straight to reporting a secret passage, the one report with no list of its own.
- The room switcher while reporting now has a search box too.

## [1.17.6] - 2026-11-20

- Tapping the version number opens the release notes.
- Sheets now close with an × at the top — the grab bar is gone.
- Picked the wrong room while reporting? You can switch rooms right there now.

## [1.17.5] - 2026-11-20

- Rooms and People are now listed in full in Researcher — check them off as you visit.
- Phones, Numbers and FAZR each have a Report button at the top of their list.
- The room picker now has a ring picker above the search, like the Wayfinder.
- Removing Key of Color (not a real room).

## [1.17.4] - 2026-11-20
